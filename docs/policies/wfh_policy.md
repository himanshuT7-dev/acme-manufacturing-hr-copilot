# Acme Manufacturing - Remote Work & Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy

## Section 1: Objective and Scope
This policy establishes guidelines for remote work and Work-From-Home (WFH) arrangements at Acme Manufacturing. Because Acme is an advanced manufacturing enterprise, operational roles require physical on-site presence, whereas designated engineering, administrative, and corporate support roles may qualify for hybrid flexibility.

## Section 2: Role Eligibility
1. **On-Site Mandatory Roles**:
   - Plant floor technicians, assembly line operators, safety inspectors, tooling engineers, and warehouse logistics personnel must work 100% on-site.
2. **Hybrid / Remote Eligible Roles**:
   - Software development, supply chain planning, procurement, human resources (`hr_admin`), accounting, and documentation roles are eligible for up to 2 days of WFH per week.
3. **Probationary Employees**:
   - Employees under active probation (`on_probation: true`) must work on-site during their initial 60 days to facilitate mentoring and operational onboarding.

## Section 3: Scheduling and Approval Workflow
- Eligible employees must submit WFH schedules to their department manager at least 48 hours in advance.
- Remote work days cannot be scheduled consecutively with public holidays or extended annual leave without HR Administrator authorization.
- Core operational hours (10:00 AM to 04:00 PM local time) require active responsiveness on internal communication platforms and corporate email.

## Section 4: Technology, Equipment, and Information Security
- Employees must conduct all company business using Acme-issued hardware or secure VPN client profiles.
- Processing confidential proprietary drawings, manufacturing schematics, or unreleased HR records on non-company devices is strictly prohibited.
- High-speed internet connectivity capable of supporting video conferencing is the responsibility of the employee.

## Section 5: Performance and Review
- Remote work privilege is contingent on sustained high performance and adherence to delivery deadlines.
- Department heads reserve the right to revoke or amend remote work arrangements if team deliverables or production support schedules are compromised.
