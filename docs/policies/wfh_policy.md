# Acme Manufacturing - Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## Section 1: Eligibility
- Confirmed employees working in Accounts, Sales, and HR departments are eligible to apply for Work From Home (WFH).
- Employees in Production roles and employees currently on probation (`on_probation: true`) are not eligible for Work From Home due to operational and training requirements.

## Section 2: Monthly Limits and Advance Notice
- Eligible employees may avail a maximum of 4 WFH days per calendar month.
- All WFH requests must be applied at least 1 day in advance through the HR portal.

## Section 3: Availability and Core Hours
- Employees working from home must remain fully reachable via phone, email, and internal chat between 10:00 and 17:00.
- Failure to respond during core hours may lead to cancellation of WFH privileges.

## Section 4: Public Holidays and Long Weekends
- Work From Home is strictly not permitted on the working day immediately before or immediately after a public holiday.
