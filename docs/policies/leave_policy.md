# Acme Manufacturing - Leave Policy

## Section 1: Leave Types and Entitlements
Acme Manufacturing provides three categories of leave per calendar year (1 January to 31 December):
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 8 days per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 6 days per calendar year.
- **Earned Leave (EL)**: 15 days per calendar year.

Employees on probation (`on_probation: true`) are entitled to Casual Leave (CL) and Sick Leave (SL) only. Probationary employees are not eligible for Earned Leave (EL) until successful completion of probation and confirmation.

## Section 2: Applying for Leave
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: Must be applied at least 1 day in advance. A maximum of 3 consecutive days of Casual Leave can be taken at a time.
- **Earned Leave (EL)**: Must be applied at least 7 days in advance.
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: Can be applied on the same day in case of sudden illness or medical emergency.

## Section 3: Carry Forward of Leave
- Unused Earned Leave (EL) carries forward to the following calendar year up to a maximum of 30 days.
- Unused Casual Leave (CL) and Sick Leave (SL) cannot be carried forward and lapse automatically on 31 December each year.

## Section 4: Leave Encashment
- Accumulated Earned Leave (EL) above the maximum 30-day carry-forward ceiling is paid out at year-end at the employee's basic daily rate.
- Casual Leave (CL) and Sick Leave (SL) are non-encashable.

## Section 5: Medical Certificate Requirement
- For Sick Leave (SL) exceeding 2 consecutive days, a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory.
- The certificate must be submitted to HR within 3 days of returning to duty.

## Section 6: Sandwich Rule
- If an employee is on approved or unapproved leave on both a Friday and the following Monday, the intervening weekend days (Saturday and Sunday) shall be counted as leave and deducted from the employee's leave balance.
