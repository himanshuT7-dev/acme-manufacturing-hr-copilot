# Acme Manufacturing - Employee Leave Policy

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the guidelines and entitlements governing employee leaves at Acme Manufacturing. It applies to all permanent, full-time, part-time, and probationary employees across administrative, engineering, and plant manufacturing operations.

## Section 2: Leave Categories and Entitlements
Acme Manufacturing provides the following annual leave categories:
1. **Annual Leave (Privilege/Earned Leave)**:
   - Full-time permanent employees accrue up to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
   - Intended for rest, relaxation, personal travel, and scheduled family commitments.
2. **Sick Leave**:
   - Employees are entitled to up to 10 days of paid sick leave annually.
   - Absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days require a medical certificate from a licensed physician upon resumption of duties.
3. **Casual / Emergency Leave**:
   - Employees are granted up to 6 days of casual leave for urgent personal matters that cannot be scheduled in advance.
   - Maximum consecutive casual leave permitted is 2 days.
4. **Unpaid Leave / Leave Without Pay (LWP)**:
   - Permitted only after all accrued paid leaves are exhausted and with formal approval from Department Head and HR Admin.

## Section 3: Probationary Period Restrictions
- Employees on probation (`on_probation: true`) are not eligible to utilize Annual Leave during their first 90 days of employment.
- Probationary employees may access Sick Leave (pro-rated) and emergency Casual Leave with HR supervisor sign-off.
- Any unauthorized absence during probation extends the probationary evaluation period.

## Section 4: Application and Advance Notice Requirements
- **Planned Leaves (>3 consecutive days)**: Must be applied at least 14 days in advance via the HR portal.
- **Short Planned Leaves (1 to 2 days)**: Must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance.
- **Emergency Leaves / Sudden Sickness**: Employee or their representative must notify the shift supervisor at least 30 minutes before their scheduled `shift_start`.

## Section 5: Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation and Approval Workflow
- When submitting leave requests via the HR Copilot or self-service interface, all applications require explicit user confirmation (`confirmed: true`) after verifying balance sufficiency and dates.
- Requests exceeding 5 consecutive working days require secondary sign-off from the Department Head and HR Administrator.

## Section 6: Leave Encashment and Carryover
- Up to 8 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.
- Unused sick leaves and casual leaves lapse at the end of each calendar year and cannot be en-cashed.
